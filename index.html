About

Hello, I'm Dr. Milo Artunduaga.

I'm board certified in both adult and child & adolescent psychiatry. In practice, that means I've spent years studying how people become who they are — and I bring that lens to every conversation.

When you tell me what's hard right now, I'm also listening for the story of how you got here, and what a next chapter could realistically look like. Most of the people I work with are adolescents, young adults, and adults in the middle of a transition: a new city or job, a diagnosis that finally makes sense of things, an identity coming into focus, a relationship ending or beginning. I have a long-standing commitment to LGBTQIA+ patients and to gender-affirming care.

My approach is collaborative. We'll draw on evidence-based psychotherapies — cognitive behavioral, family-based, insight-oriented — and when medication can help, I'll explain exactly why I'm recommending it and what we should expect from it. Small, consistent changes, made together, are how people rise.

— Milo

Services

How we might work together.

Psychiatric evaluation

A thorough first look at what's going on — your history, your context, and what you want to be different. You leave with a clear picture and a plan, whether or not we continue together.

Fit

Who I'm right for — and who I'm not.

I want you to get the best care available, and sometimes that isn't with me. So that you can work with clinicians who focus on exactly what you need, I refer out:

  • Patients over 65 — geriatric psychiatry is its own specialty, and you deserve someone who practices it every day.
  • Anyone currently needing active treatment for substance dependence, where a dedicated program will serve you far better than a solo practice can.

If that's you, please still reach out. I'm glad to point you toward good people.

Fees & getting started

Out-of-network, on purpose.

I don't contract with insurance companies so that the length and frequency of our visits are decided by what you need, not by a billing code. After each visit you'll receive a superbill, and many PPO plans reimburse a meaningful portion.
